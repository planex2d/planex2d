osifont-lgpl3fe.ttf

osifont, a font after ISO 3098 (the lettering DIN EN ISO 3098-2 asks for on technical drawings), version
0.1.20221020, by Zefram Cochrane (hikikomori82@gmail.com) and the osifont contributors,
https://github.com/hikikomori82/osifont, the file osifont-lgpl3fe.ttf of commit 2e9aa86a8a09 (2026-04-16), unmodified.

planex's px.Drawing and px.Sheet write their lettering in it (px.Sheet(..., font=...) takes another TrueType file).
