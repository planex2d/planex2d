Areas, centroids and second moments

The section properties of a profile, its area, centroid and second moments of area, are what a beam calculation needs. center is the centroid in 3D, and Measure() adds the second moments about it in the plane of the object:

  • Ih, the integral of v², about the h axis;

  • Iv, the integral of h², about the v axis;

  • Ihv, the product moment, the integral of h v;

  • I1 ≥ I2, the principal second moments, and principal_angle, the angle of the first principal axis in degrees.

[1]:

import math
import planex as px

Everything at once

Measure() returns every measurement of an object as a dict. The example is an IPE 200 beam section without its root radii: 200 high and 100 wide, with flanges 8.5 thick and a web 5.6 thick (Figure 1, with the centroid marked).

[3]:

flange = px.QuickSketch().RectangleC(100, 8.5).Face()
web = px.QuickSketch().RectangleC(5.6, 200 - 2 * 8.5).Face()
beam = px.Unify([flange.Translated((0, 95.75)), web, flange.Translated((0, -95.75))])

[5]:

m = beam.Measure()
for key in ["type", "area", "center", "Ih", "Iv", "I1", "I2", "principal_angle", "bounding_box"]:
    print(key, m[key])

Two sections

The first example is a T-slot nut, a flange of 18 × 4 below a neck of 10 × 6, with its centroid marked (Figure 2a). Its half is drawn and mirrored. The centroid of two rectangles lies at v = (A1 v1 + A2 v2) / (A1 + A2).

[6]:

half = px.QuickSketch().Line(9).Line(0, 4).Line(-4, 0).Line(0, 6).Line((0, 10)).Close().Face()
nut = half + half.Mirrored((0, 0), (0, 1))
A1, A2 = 18 * 4, 10 * 6
print("nut centroid", nut.center, "exact v", (A1 * 2 + A2 * (4 + 3)) / (A1 + A2))

nut centroid (1.0765799026668185e-16, 4.2727272727272725, 0.0) exact v 4.2727272727272725

The second example is a sharp-cornered equal angle of 40 × 40 × 5, with its centroid and its principal axes dashed (Figure 2b). The principal axes lie at 45° to the legs. Its second moments follow from the two rectangles about the outer corner, moved to the centroid. With L = 40 and t = 5:

[7]:

L, t = 40, 5
angle = px.QuickSketch().Line(L).Line(0, t).Line(t - L, 0).Line(0, L - t).Line(-t, 0).Close().Face()
m = angle.Measure()

A = t * (2 * L - t)
c = (L**2 + L * t - t**2) / (2 * (2 * L - t))
I = (t * L**3 + (L - t) * t**3) / 3 - A * c**2
Ihv = t**2 * (2 * L**2 - t**2) / 4 - A * c**2
for key, exact in {"area": A, "Ih": I, "Iv": I, "I1": I + abs(Ihv), "I2": I - abs(Ihv)}.items():
    print(key, m[key], "exact", exact)
print("principal angle", m["principal_angle"])
