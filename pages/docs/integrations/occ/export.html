To OCC shapes

Faces, wires and sketches become OpenCASCADE shapes with exact lines, arcs, full circles and B-splines, shared vertices, names and colors. The export needs OpenCASCADE through netgen.occ (pip install netgen-mesher, which brings OpenCASCADE as netgen-occt). On the way out, the export can apply everything that Simplify does. See Defeaturing for details.

The rocket example is revolved, lofted and extruded from planex2d sketches.

The example is a plate 4 × 2 with a bore of radius 0.5, with its outline and bore named (Figure 1):

[1]:

import planex as px

plate = px.QuickSketch((-2, -1)).Rectangle(4, 2, name="outline").Face()
part = plate - px.QuickSketch().Circle(0.5, name="bore").Face()
shape = part.ToOCCShape()
print(len(shape.edges), "edges:", sorted({e.name for e in shape.edges}))

5 edges: ['bore', 'outline']

Faces and wires

A face, its wires, or a wire on its own can each be exported as OpenCASCADE objects. The example exports the plate as a face and as its wires, and a slot of width 1 along a spine of 3 as one wire:

[4]:

face = part.ToOCCFace()
wires = part.ToOCCWires()
slot = px.QuickSketch().Slot(3, 1).Wire().ToOCCWire()
print(type(face).__name__, len(wires.edges), len(slot.edges))

Face 5 4
