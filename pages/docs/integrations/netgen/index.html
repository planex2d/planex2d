netgen and NGSolve

A potential flow on the mesh of a planex2d face A cold plate's temperature as a measurement An auxetic lattice from a level set, loaded

planex2d faces and shapes become netgen geometries in one call. The geometry reaches netgen through OpenCASCADE, in the build that netgen ships as netgen-occt, so everything on the OpenCASCADE pages applies here too, and every plane argument takes netgen’s occ.Axes besides a px.Plane. Face names turn into materials, edge names into boundary conditions, and vertex names into point names. maxh and hpref on faces, edges and vertices set the mesh size and refine towards corners. Faces glued into a shape share their edges, so the regions of the mesh fit together without gaps. Results come back onto the geometry: px.PlotField draws NGSolve and FEniCSx fields on the exact planex2d geometry, also in cuts through 3D parts. A measurement can pass the part to netgen for meshing and to NGSolve for solving, and px.FromLevelSet turns a level set function into a planex2d shape.
